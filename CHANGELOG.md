### Added
  - `MET-WP1-07`: establish the Metrics rollup-delta persistence foundation on the `feature/metrics` programme integration branch (issue [880](https://github.com/thoth-pub/thoth/issues/880)). Adds migration `20260903_v1.9.0` creating the single `metric_rollup_delta` table: the durable accounting bridge between one canonical metric-record revision and the rebuildable work-level rollup projections, holding `delta_id`, `record_id`, `revision_id`, `delta_value`, `status`, `created_at` and `applied_at`. `delta_value` is a signed `BIGINT` with deliberately **no** non-negative CHECK, because a revision contributes the signed difference `new - old` and a retraction subtracts the previously applied value, so positive, zero and negative deltas are all valid. `status` is required `TEXT` rather than a PostgreSQL enum and carries no CHECK enumerating values, because the approved design names the field but defines no closed status vocabulary, transition model, claim ownership, lease or recovery protocol — inventing one here would pre-decide the later WP4 claim/application state machine; `applied_at` correspondingly stays nullable with no cross-column invariant tying it to `status`, and no trigger or stored procedure moves a delta between states. A single non-cascading composite foreign key over `(record_id, revision_id)` against the merged `MET-WP1-04` `metric_record_revision (record_id, record_revision_id)` unique key guarantees a delta cannot pair one record with a revision owned by another record, and means deleting a referenced record or revision fails instead of silently erasing durable accounting evidence; `UNIQUE(revision_id)` permits at most one delta per canonical revision so later rollup application cannot double count. Deliberately carries no operational claim index such as `(status, created_at)` beyond the primary key and the revision-uniqueness index, because an access path must not be encoded before the WP4 claim query and its query-plan evidence are approved. Updates the manually maintained `thoth-api/src/schema.rs` contract atomically with the migration under `ADR-0003`, adds the `metric_rollup_delta` Rust domain module and focused database/model tests, including null and non-null `applied_at` round-trips, positive, zero, negative and extreme signed delta values, arbitrary `status` text, the complete authorized column/CHECK/foreign-key/index inventory, composite same-record and duplicate-revision rejection, restricted non-cascading deletion, and targeted revert-through rollback/reapply of the rollup-delta migration via the embedded harness, leaving the `MET-WP1-01`/`02`/`03`/`04`/`05`/`06` schema and seeded measures byte-identical. Additive and inactive: no delta row is seeded, and the slice implements no delta generation from ingestion, no first-arrival/revision/retraction transaction, no delta claiming, lease, retry, backoff or stale-claim recovery, no `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` application loop, no delta application, no rollup projection table (`metric_rollup_work_day`, `metric_rollup_work_month`, `metric_rollup_work_country_month` and `metric_rollup_work_institution_month` remain approved future architecture), no rebuild generation or active-watermark behaviour, no `claimMetricRollupDeltas`/`completeMetricRollupDeltas` mutation, no dashboard query, no GraphQL, authorization or administration surface and no production migration. WP1 remains in progress.

Adds migration `20260831_v1.9.0` creating the two closed enums `metric_record_revision_status` (`CURRENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `RETRACTED`) and `metric_record_provenance_classification` (`WINNER`, `DUPLICATE`, `REVISION`, `CONFLICT`, `REJECTED`), and the three canonical history tables `metric_record` (the stable identity of one reporting cell), `metric_record_revision` (immutable value revisions) and `metric_record_provenance` (durable evidence for every normalized source row, including rejected and conflicting rows, hence its intentionally nullable `record_id`).
