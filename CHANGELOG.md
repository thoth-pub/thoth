Adds migration `20260831_v1.9.0` creating the two closed enums `metric_record_revision_status` (`CURRENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `RETRACTED`) and `metric_record_provenance_classification` (`WINNER`, `DUPLICATE`, `REVISION`, `CONFLICT`, `REJECTED`), and the three canonical history tables `metric_record` (the stable identity of one reporting cell), `metric_record_revision` (immutable value revisions) and `metric_record_provenance` (durable evidence for every normalized source row, including rejected and conflicting rows, hence its intentionally nullable `record_id`). Canonical identity is carried by a unique non-blank `identity_hash` rather than a natural composite key, because it excludes both the acquisition route and the value; no publisher, imprint or series attribution is stored on the record. The intentionally circular record/current-revision relationship is created additively — `metric_record` first with a nullable `current_revision_id`, then `metric_record_revision`, then the constraint — and both same-record invariants are enforced declaratively with **composite foreign keys** over `(record_id, current_revision_id)` and `(record_id, supersedes_revision_id)` against a supporting `(record_id, record_revision_id)` unique key, so neither pointer can name a revision owned by another record, with no trigger and no new dependency. Adds a partial unique index permitting at most one `CURRENT` revision per record, `UNIQUE(record_id, revision_number)` with `revision_number > 0`, half-open period ordering (`period_end > period_start`), a **separate** nullable Metrics alpha-2 `CHAR(2)` `country_code` accepting exactly two uppercase ASCII letters that neither reuses nor changes the existing bibliographic alpha-3 `CountryCode` enum, non-blank `identity_hash`/`content_hash` checks carrying no algorithm, encoding or length rule, non-cascading foreign keys to `work`, `publication`, `metric_platform`, `metric_measure`, `institution`, `metric_source_account` and `metric_import`, reuse of the existing `metric_reporting_grain` enum, generic non-null JSONB `details` defaulting to `{}`, repository-standard UUID and current-time defaults, and exactly the four design-required `metric_record` access indexes plus the three provenance audit indexes. Revision `value` stays a signed `BIGINT` with deliberately no blanket non-negative constraint, because sales measures may report signed net units. Updates the manually maintained `thoth-api/src/schema.rs` contract atomically with the migration under `ADR-0003`, adds the `metric_record`, `metric_record_revision` and `metric_record_provenance` Rust domain modules and focused database/model tests. Additive and inactive: no row is seeded, and the slice implements no identity or content hashing, no normalized-observation validation, no DOI/ISBN/ROR resolution, no first-arrival, duplicate, revision, conflict or retraction transaction, no managed-source revision authorization, no publisher finality, no rollup delta, no period-overlap detection or concurrency protocol (no exclusion constraint, `btree_gist` or advisory lock), no GraphQL, authorization or administration surface and no production migration. ### Added
  - `API-IMPORT-ORCID-BATCH-01`: additive `contributorsByOrcids(orcids: [Orcid!]!): [Contributor!]!` query resolving a bounded set of ORCIDs to contributors with a single set-based exact-equality lookup, allowing bulk-import clients to avoid one request per ORCID (issue [851](https://github.com/thoth-pub/thoth/issues/851), parent [850](https://github.com/thoth-pub/thoth/issues/850)). Values must be supplied in Thoth's stored representation (`https://orcid.org/XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`); matching is exact, so partial identifiers never match, unknown ORCIDs are omitted, and each matching contributor is returned at most once. At most 1000 ORCIDs per request: a larger list returns a clear field error without truncating or querying the database, and an empty list returns `[]` without querying it either. `contributors(filter:)`, `contributor(id:)`, contributor writes, ORCID uniqueness, history and authorization are unchanged.
